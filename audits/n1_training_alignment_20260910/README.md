## Method

The easiest available independent reference is the NOVA eigenmode-run log.
`out_go` and `out_go_prev` record expected singularity radii for each
`hhh,om` frequency, which is omega squared. Match to the full binary-header
frequency at relative tolerance 1e-12, not the rounded filename. Compare each
shared-loader datcon crossing with the nearest logged singularity:

```
offset_r = r_datcon_crossing - r_nearest_logged_singularity
offset_grid = offset_r * (nr - 1)
```

The primary screen uses `0.03 <= r < 0.75`, excluding the axis and the crowded
outer spectrum. Every measured mode is nr=201, so two grid intervals are
0.01 in normalized radius. The two-interval cutoff is a useful review
tolerance, not a calibrated new validity gate. Report absolute offset,
fraction beyond the tolerance, branch, frequency, and coverage; opposite
offsets at different crossings can cancel in a signed mean. Seek repetition
across several modes/frequencies, then inspect signed profiles for confirmation.

The log parser requires a complete singularity block paired to its own
frequency. It does not carry a previous block forward. Matching frequencies
with conflicting lists, empty lists, missing frequencies and input failures
have separate statuses. All 1,567 scanned N1/N2 modes have zero parser-conflict,
incomplete-block or input-error cases. Four focused tests check these semantics.

The logged radii agree with `(ising-1)/(nr-1)` to 1.2e-16 in this run. That
confirms the radial coordinate used in the comparison. Logs can include
other continuum branches, however, and a nearest singularity is not a
branch identification. A large nearest distance is useful evidence of an
inconsistency; a small distance alone does not certify alignment. Missing
nearby singularities must not be interpreted as an enormous measured shift
of a specifically identified resonance.

## Scope and results

Read the canonical training data under `data_mixed`, using logs in the live
DiTw shot directories. Scanned all N1/N2 mode files in the 14 active training
shots: **990 N1 and 577 N2 modes**. The active list contains **194 N1 modes**:
142 have matching nonempty singularity lists, 14 have matching empty lists,
and 38 have no exact-frequency record. Of the 142, **127 modes provide 153
interior crossings**. Every one of those 142 training mode files is byte
identical to the corresponding file in the log directory.

The primary training N1 findings are:

| Shot | Informative modes | Interior crossings beyond 2 intervals | Median absolute offset (intervals) | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| nstx_135388 | 30 | 38 / 41 | 4.90 | Strong repeated lower-boundary mismatch; some upper-boundary crossings align. |
| G142301W29 | 14 | 16 / 16 | 6.49 | All 14 outer upper-boundary crossings are displaced outwards by 5.12–10.96 intervals. Two extra near-axis lower crossings have no nearby log counterpart. |
| G142301Y93 | 11 | 21 / 22 | 5.49 | Repeated discrepancies on both inner and outer crossings; directions can differ. |
| nstx_141711 | 10 | 5 / 10 | 1.84 | Smaller inward offsets, plus one missing nearby log counterpart; secondary review. |
| E204669M03t025 | 28 | 0 / 28 | 0.55 | No systematic offset found in informative training modes. |
| E205052A01t022 | 31 | 1 / 31 | 1.22 | No widespread offset beyond the review tolerance. |

Signed-profile checks support the first three flags:

- `135388/N1/2067`: lower crossing r=0.473913 versus log r=0.455;
  the sharp profile change is near the log radius. For `2424`, the two
  lower crossings lie near 0.141/0.390 versus log radii 0.170/0.360.
  `6037` supplies a within-N1 comparison with an upper crossing aligned
  to within one interval. This is not a rigid translation of all branches.
- `W29/N1/2158` and `2503`: sharp structure near log r=0.685 and 0.600;
  datcon upper crossings are r=0.718422 and 0.654794. The two offsets are
  6.68 and 10.96 intervals.
- `Y93/N1/1894` and `2168`: outer sharp features follow logged radii near
  0.685/0.645 rather than datcon crossings near 0.704/0.677. Inner
  discrepancies occur in the opposite direction in these examples.

Other evidence and coverage limits:

- **B12:** both N1 training modes disagree strongly with the log. N2 also
  has large discrepancies, so this does not isolate an N1-only problem.
  Some nearest matches are distant unrelated singularities. Plots show why
  the large numbers should be treated as missing correspondence, not a
  measured translation of a known branch.
- **H47:** its one N1 training mode has a datcon crossing but the matched
  log reports zero singularities. This is a separate sparse disagreement.
  N2 also warrants attention (10/12 training interior crossings exceed two
  intervals). This screen cannot certify an entire N1 calculation from one mode.
- **120113:** 37 of 38 training frequencies are absent from available logs;
  the one matched crossing is within 1.04 intervals. The wider raw population
  has aligned log comparisons, and three training examples spanning the
  frequency range visually place sharp structure near datcon crossings.
  This is supporting evidence, not complete validation of the 37 missing records.
- **E202855A01t020:** all six N1 training modes have neither datcon crossings
  nor logged singularities. They supply no resonance-location test.
- **J38:** nine N1 training modes have matching logs but no crossing in the
  selected interior region. Outer crossings remain in the full runtime CSV.
- **K51, S31, nstxu_204202:** no active N1 training rows. The latter also has
  no raw N1 modes in the canonical training tree.
- N2 is a control, not an assumed correct reference. Besides B12 and H47,
  W29 has 11/16 training interior crossings beyond two intervals, J38 3/3,
  and K51 9/9. Y93 has 7/40, with closer alignment in the plotted N2 example.
  135388 and 120113 have no usable N2 frequency matches in the available logs.
  These findings do not identify whether continuum or eigenmode-run inputs
  are wrong, nor justify an N1-only automatic exclusion for every flagged shot.

The shared continuum repair changes **zero samples in the selected interior
region** for all 13 populated N1 directories. Thus it does not cause the
reported offsets. No raw inputs, training labels, known-invalid registry,
sorter thresholds, models or production output trees were changed.
