# E202926A03t025 N3/1151 and N4/3735: narrow-envelope rejections

User queried these two BAD decisions after adoption of production v12.
Both are rejected only by `BAD_INTERIOR_UNRESOLVED_ENVELOPE`, which already
rejected them in v11. The new distributed harmonic noise gate does not fire.
This is a diagnostic review, not a label override.

The gate measures the connected full width at half maximum of unsmoothed
`W(r)=sum_m |xi_m(r)|^2` around its global peak. It rejects at peak r<=0.5
and energy FWHM<=2 grid intervals, unless a matched continuum extremum
satisfies both `ext_dr<=0.02` and `0.001<ext_df_gap<=0.04`.
The strict lower clearance bound is **0.1% of mode frequency**.

Both match an extremum radially, but neither passes the 0.1% clearance
floor. The amplitude-lobe measurements above use the dominant stored row
at the energy peak (row 6 for N3, row 8 for N4). They are supplemental;
the envelope gate uses energy width, not amplitude width or integrated
whole-mode radial extent. Signed profiles show sharp main peaks at the
extrema, with wider lobes and tails; N4 also has substantial outer structure.
The small energy FWHM therefore does not imply that the entire mode is
confined to that narrow interval.

No other enabled gate fires. N3 has no true continuum crossings. N4 has a
lower crossing at r=0.0646218, far from the main peak, with window amplitude
0.0123601 and normalized window energy 0.00008248, both below their cuts.
For N4, the standard RF extremum feature has no match because its search
ends at r=0.4; the rule-specific search reaches r=0.5 and finds the match
reported here. Missing extremum detection is not the reason for rejection.

The current floor deliberately excludes these very close-to-extremum narrow
peaks. Accepting them would require reconsidering that rule or an explicit
adjudication; this audit does not establish that the narrow peaks are numerical.
No gates, labels, or installed production outputs were changed.
